# Goals

## Core Game
- Create a retro pixel-art Kaiju autobattler.
- Player builds and modifies a Kaiju in a laboratory.
- Battles are mostly automatic.
- Kaiju moves through 2.5D scrolling levels.
- Enemies attack throughout the level.
- Each level ends with a boss.

## Progression
- Defeated enemies provide resources.
- Kaiju returns to the laboratory after battle.
- Player can replace organs and body parts.
- Builds should allow very different playstyles.
- Every deployment ends in a meaningful salvage decision rather than only automatic currency gain.
- Completing both available biomes forms a repeatable campaign circuit with rising threat and rewards.

## Design Goals
- Battles should last roughly 2–5 minutes.
- Player decisions happen primarily before battle.
- Runs should encourage experimentation with builds.
- The laboratory should explain a build's archetype, strengths, dependencies, and metabolic liabilities before deployment.

## Current Product Tranche

- Complete a persistent post-battle salvage choice flow.
- Expand buildcraft beyond one arm socket into brain, heart, stomach, and torso alternatives.
- Add a replayable City Ruins / Toxic Swamp campaign circuit with data-driven unlocks and threat scaling.
