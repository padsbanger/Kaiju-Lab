# Product Goals

- Make pre-deployment anatomy choices the player's main form of agency.
- Model energy, blood, oxygen, heat, and biomass as understandable biological systems.
- Keep battles autonomous, readable, and roughly 2–5 minutes at normal speed.
- Return the same wounded specimen to the lab after every deployment.
- Support meaningful organ alternatives instead of flat equipment upgrades.
- Deliver a crisp 640×360 pixel-art presentation using only native Godot 2D gameplay nodes.
